                          Present and Tardy each count as one present day; Absent counts as one absent day. Each recorded month shows separate Present and Absent totals, followed by overall totals.

                          Formulas: Df = learners who answered correctly ÷ total learners. Ds = proportion correct in the Upper Group − proportion correct in the Lower Group. Upper and Lower Groups are the top and bottom 27% by total test score.

                          Interpretation: Df 0.20–0.80 and Ds ≥ 0.30 = Retain; Df 0.20–0.80 and Ds 0.20–0.29 = Revise; Df below 0.20, Ds below 0.20, or negative Ds = Revise or Discard; Df above 0.80 with Ds ≥ 0.30 = Retain if still useful. Distractors should attract more learners from the Lower Group; non-functional distractors should be flagged.

                          RMA (Rapid Mathematics Assessment)

                          Record BOSY, MOSY, and EOSY math results, review mastery, and track learner progress.

                          Template scoring: total score is the sum of weighted item points (items may be worth 1, 2, or 3 points); percentage score = total score ÷ the selected grade-level maximum (Grade 4: 13, Grade 5: 32, Grade 6: 50) × 100. Levelling uses the template’s grade-specific raw-score cutoffs. Content and cognitive domain benchmarks are met at 75% or higher. A learner is counted as assessed only when an assessment date is entered.
